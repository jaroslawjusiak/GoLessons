# Go Course Syllabus: From 30 Kyu to 10 Kyu

**Target Audience:** Absolute beginners to intermediate players  
**Setting:** Local Youth Cultural Centre Go Club  
**Format:** Weekly 4-hour sessions (15–20 minute lecture + SGF review / guided practice / tsumego solving)  
**Total Duration:** 30 meetings (3 Phases: 9x9, 13x13, 19x19)

## Course Overview

### Goal

Take a complete beginner (30 kyu) to a solid intermediate level (~10 kyu) over one year of weekly meetings.

### Key Milestones

* **Phase 1 (Weeks 1–4): 9x9 Board — Fundamental Rules & Life/Death Basics**
  * *Goal:* Understand liberties, capturing, two eyes, basic life and death, and 9x9 tactical completion (30k → 22k).
* **Phase 2 (Weeks 5–14): 13x13 Board — Opening Strategy, Shapes & Fundamental Tactics**
  * *Goal:* Transition to full-board awareness, understand opening principles (Corners → Sides → Center), basic tactical tesuji, sente/gote, and strong vs. weak groups (22k → 15k).
* **Phase 3 (Weeks 15–30): 19x19 Board — Advanced Strategy, Direction of Play, Sabaki & Endgame**
  * *Goal:* Master full-scale 19x19 strategic concepts, direction of play, joseki frameworks, using influence, aji, sabaki, and systematic endgame calculation (15k → 10k).

## Phase 1: The 9x9 Board — Fundamentals & Rules (Weeks 1–4)

### Lesson 1: Introduction to Go — Rules, Liberties, Capturing techniques, Ko, Territory

* **Lecture Topics:**
  * The origin and beauty of Go.
  * Intersections, turns, placing stones on lines.
  * Liberties (Breathing points) of a single stone and connected groups.
  * Atari (Immediate threat of capture) and capturing stones.
  * The rule of suicide (Illegal moves) and the Ko rule (avoiding infinite loops - basic introduction).
  * Goal of the game: surrounding territory
  * Simple tactics:
    * The ladder (shicho) technique
    * The "net" (geta) capture

  * *Book:* Janice Kim & Jeong Soo-hyun, *Learn to Play Go: Volume 1 - The Game of Go*.
